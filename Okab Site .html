About OKAB Cattle Feed

OKAB Cattle Feed is manufactured by Velocity Feed Industries, a trusted name in animal nutrition. Based in Punjab, we understand the unique needs of Indian dairy farmers and have developed our products specifically for local conditions.

Our comprehensive range includes specialized feeds for different stages of cattle development - from calf starters to high-energy milking feeds. Each product is carefully formulated with optimal protein, fat, and mineral content to ensure maximum nutrition and productivity.

With our commitment to quality and farmer success, OKAB has become a preferred choice for dairy farmers across Punjab and neighboring regions.
